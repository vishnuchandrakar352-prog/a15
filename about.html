About the maison

We write about coats the way tailors talk about them

Frocked is an independent outerwear journal. We focus on long coats and trench coats: where their details come from, how they should fit and how to choose one that lasts.

    IndependentWe sell nothing and use no affiliate links. No label pays to be mentioned.

    ReviewedEvery guide is read by a second editor and dated when it changes.
